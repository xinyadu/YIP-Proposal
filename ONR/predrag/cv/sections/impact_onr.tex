\section{Research \\ Impact}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=10pt, label=\textbullet, itemsep=1pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=0pt]
\item \textbf{Citations.} \scholarcites{} citations, h-index \scholarhindex{}, i10-index \scholarifive{} (Google Scholar, October 2026). ``Learning to Ask'' (ACL 2017) \pubref{pub:learn2ask} has over 1,000 citations, is among Paper Digest's most influential ACL papers and the top 0.1\% most-cited computer science articles of 2017, and was featured in \emph{New Scientist} and \emph{TechRepublic}; ``Event Extraction by Answering (Almost) Natural Questions'' (EMNLP 2020) \pubref{pub:eeqa} has over 600. \item \textbf{Adoption beyond the lab.} OpenAI used DSBench \pubref{pub:dsbench} to evaluate ChatGPT agent (2025). PRD's peer evaluation of AI judges \pubref{pub:prd} has been built on by researchers at NYU, Anthropic, and Cohere; FaithScore \pubref{pub:faithscore} has been adopted by groups at Arizona State, the University of Washington, Johns Hopkins, and Intel Labs. Led the LLM usage-and-licensing section of \emph{The Practical Guides for LLMs} on GitHub (10k+ stars).
\item \textbf{Publications.} 66 papers and preprints: 5 journal articles (ACM Computing Surveys, IJCV, TMLR) and 46 peer-reviewed conference papers, 28 of them in the main proceedings of NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, and AAAI.
\item \textbf{Recognition.} NSF CAREER Award (2024, funded on first submission); Best Paper Award, AAAI 2025 AI4Research Workshop; Best Poster Award (top 1\%), ICML 2024 AI for Science Workshop; Outstanding Area Chair Award, EMNLP 2024; AAAI New Faculty Highlights (2024); Amazon (2023) and Cisco (2024) Research Awards; University of Chicago Rising Star in Data Science (2021).
\item \textbf{Systems and resources.} First author of RESIN-11 \pubref{pub:resin}; led the integration of the RESIN system in the \$10M DARPA KAIROS program, the top-ranking system in the program's evaluation. Built the PeerEval public leaderboard on PRD \pubref{pub:prd}, and benchmarks including MEQA, DSBench, LMR-Bench, and SciEvent.

\item \textbf{Funding.} \$1.02M in external research funding as sole PI (NSF CAREER, Open Philanthropy, Amazon, Cisco, Adobe, OpenAI), plus Amazon awards as co-PI and lead contributor.
\item \textbf{Mentoring and teaching.} Three Ph.D. students (two finishing in 2026) and over 20 master's, undergraduate, and K--12 students; former mentees went on to Ph.D. programs at CMU, Cornell, and NTU. An undergraduate mentee published in TMLR and won a Jonsson School award, and K--12 outreach projects led to the paper underlying the NSF CAREER award. New LLM course rated 4.75/5 (instructor) and 4.65/5 (course); President's Teaching Excellence Award nominee.

AI judges that evaluate one another (PRD \pubref{pub:prd}, IQA-EVAL \pubref{pub:iqaeval}); step-level rewards and verification (FG-PRM \pubref{pub:fgprm}, HiPRAG \pubref{pub:hiprag}, training-free verification \pubref{pub:verifier}); evaluation benchmarks (MEQA \pubref{pub:meqa}, FaithScore \pubref{pub:faithscore}, LMR-Bench \pubref{pub:lmrbench}); red-teaming of code models (COMET \pubref{pub:comet}); and AI for science (LDC \pubref{pub:ldc}, LLM4SR \pubref{pub:llm4sr}).
